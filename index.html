About Ilona

✂️

Hi, I'm Ilona — a Ukrainian hairstylist based in Calgary, with over 10 years of experience behind the chair.

I specialize in haircuts, color, and bridal hair. My goal is simple: hair that looks beautiful in real life, not just in photos. Whether it's a fresh cut, a full color transformation, or your wedding day look, every appointment starts with a consultation — so you leave with hair you love, and a plan to keep it that way.
